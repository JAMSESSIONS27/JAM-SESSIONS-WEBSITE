# Privacy Policy (Draft)

Last updated: [YYYY-MM-DD]

This privacy policy explains how JAM SESSIONS collects, uses, and protects personal information from donors, volunteers, and visitors to our website.

Contact: kenhill4591@gmail.com

1. Information we collect
- Information you provide directly: name, email, mailing address, phone number, payment details (processed by third-party payment processors), and any message or communications you send.
- Automated data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and analytics data.

2. How we use your information
- To process donations and send receipts/acknowledgements.
- To communicate about your donation, membership, or volunteer activities when you opt in.
- To comply with legal obligations and recordkeeping for tax and accounting purposes.

3. Third-party processors
Payment processing is handled by third-party services (e.g., PayPal, Stripe, GiveLively). We do not store full payment card data on our servers. Review each processor's privacy policy for details on how they handle payment information.

4. Data retention
We retain donor records and transaction data for a minimum of 7 years to comply with tax and accounting practices, unless a different retention period is required by law. We will delete or anonymize data upon request where applicable, subject to legal or accounting retention requirements.

5. Your rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To request changes or deletion, contact: kenhill4591@gmail.com

6. Security
We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. While we strive to protect your data, no website or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children
Our site is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

8. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated date at the top of the policy.

Legal note: This is a draft privacy policy. Please have it reviewed and approved by legal counsel before publishing.
